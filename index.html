Case Studies

Selected outcomes that reflect our approach to complex, high-stakes matters.

CROSS-BORDER • DISPUTE PREVENTION

Wilson vs. Jon – Risk Neutralized

A senior USA-based consultant faced serious allegations including fraud, fund misappropriation, exclusivity breach, and criminal escalation with cross-border arbitration exposure.

KOSEN applied a precise contract-first strategy, established independent contractor status, and delivered an evidence-backed defence that resulted in every allegation being formally dropped — with no lawsuit, no arbitration, and no reputational damage.

RESULT: All allegations dropped without litigation
INDONESIA • MARKET ENTRY

Complex Wholesale Distribution Agreement

A leading European retail group needed a high-value wholesale distribution agreement in Indonesia, with complex SNI regulatory compliance requirements.

KOSEN structured the full SNI compliance framework, including product testing, factory audits, labeling requirements, and post-certification obligations — delivering a balanced agreement that protected commercial interests while ensuring smooth market entry.

RESULT: Full regulatory compliance + reduced market entry risk
GLOBAL • TEMPLATE DEVELOPMENT

Global Distribution Agreement & Deal Memo Templates

A leading European retail group wanted standardized tools to accelerate international deal-making while maintaining consistent legal protection across markets.

KOSEN developed a comprehensive Distribution Agreement template and a practical Deal Memo template. These are now the group’s standard foundation for all new distribution partnerships globally.

RESULT: Dramatically reduced drafting time with consistent global protection
