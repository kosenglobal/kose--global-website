Sign in with your kosen.law Google account

The CRM could not open inside this page. This usually means one of these:

  • You are not signed in to your kosen.law Google account in this browser yet.
  • Your browser blocks Google sign-in inside other websites. Safari, iPhone and iPad always do this, and Firefox often does.
  • You are signed in to more than one Google account and Google wants you to pick one.
Sign in with Google (new tab)

The new tab opens the CRM at its Google address and works in every browser. After you sign in there, come back and press Retry. On Safari and iPhone, keep using the new tab: the CRM cannot appear inside this page there.
